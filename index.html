Path of Exile 2 — Tablet Modifier Guide

Source: LilBotQ Tablet Crafting notes · Trade links target league Forbidden Rites

General Info

  • Suffix modifiers drive tablet value far more than prefixes — always prioritize suffixes.
  • Each tablet type usually has one "standout" suffix that disproportionately increases price.
  • Stacking multiple good modifiers on one tablet can multiply sale price 3–4x over a single-mod tablet.
  • Higher rolls (and divined tablets) sell for more — don't divine speculatively yourself.

Universal Prefixes (valuable on every tablet type)

  • Map has #% increased Monster Rarity
  • #% increased Rarity of Items found in Map
  • Map has #% increased number of Rare Monsters
  • Monsters have #% increased Effectiveness
Monster Rarity|Rarity of Items|Effectiveness

Always Look Out For (can roll on any tablet type)

  • Map has # additional random Modifier Standout
  • Map contains # additional Azmeri Spirit
  • Map contains # additional Strongboxes
random Modifier|Azmeri Spirit|Strongbox

Expedition Tablet

Verisium Remnant chance is the standout mod
1Expeditions have +(30–40)% Surpassing chance to contain an additional Verisium Remnant. Standout
2Expeditions contain (1–2) buried Strongboxes in Map.
3Map contains 1 additional Azmeri Spirit.
Supporting-only (need other mods to matter): Additional Verisium Sentry · Chance for Verisium Remnants to add an additional Runic Modifier.
Stash search regex
Verisium Remnant|buried|Azmeri Spirit Trade search — Forbidden Rites

Ritual Tablet

Additional rerolls is the standout mod — worth a lot
1Ritual Altars in Map allow rerolling Favours (1–3) additional times. Standout
2Ritual Favours in Map have (35–70)% increased chance to be Omens.
3Rerolling Favours at Ritual Altars in Map costs (20–30)% reduced Tribute.
4Deferring Favours at Ritual Altars in Map costs (20–30)% reduced Tribute.
Stash search regex
additional time|Omens|Rerolling|Deferring Trade search — Forbidden Rites

Abyss Tablet

No single standout — order below is inferred, not explicit in the source
1Map has (20–40)% chance to contain four additional Abysses.
2(2–3) additional Rare Monsters are spawned from Abysses in Map.
3(20–30)% increased chance for Abyssal monsters in Map to have Abyssal Modifiers.
4(20–30)% increased chance for Desecrated Currency from Abysses in Map.
5Abyss Pits in Map are twice as likely to have Rewards.
Stash search regex
four additional|spawned from|Abyssal|Desecrated Trade search — Forbidden Rites

Delirium Tablet

No standout mod — order below is inferred, not explicit in the source
1Delirium Encounters in Map are (15–30)% more likely to spawn Unique Bosses.
2Delirium Fog in Map spawns (12–26)% increased Mirror Shards.
3Delirium Fog in Map applies (15–30)% increased Deliriousness to Players.
4(15–30)% increased Stack size of Simulacrum Splinters found in Map.

Breach Tablet

Check current party-play breach strategies — prices swing with meta
1Unstable Breaches in Map spawn (1–2) additional Rare Monsters when Stabilised.
2(5–20)% increased Effectiveness of Rare Breach Monsters in Map.
3(30–60)% increased Quantity of Hiveblood found in Map.
Stash search regex
when Stabilised|Rare Breach Monsters|Hiveblood Trade search — Forbidden Rites

Irradiated Tablet

No unique suffixes — value comes from universal prefixes
1Map has # additional random Modifier.
2The four universal prefixes (Monster Rarity, Rarity of Items, Rare Monster count, Monster Effectiveness) — good prefixes alone can sell for 1 divine on these.
Stash search regex
(?=.*Irradiated)(?=.*(random Mod|Rarity|Effect)) Trade search — Forbidden Rites

Overseer Tablet

Strongbox + Azmeri Spirit stacking is extremely valuable

3Map contains an additional Azmeri Spirit.

Temple Tablet

Vaal Beacon & unique monster modifiers
1(5–10)% chance to gain an additional Crystal from Vaal Beacons in Map.
2Unique Monsters have 1 additional Rare Modifier.
3#% chance to add a Vaal Beacon Unique Monster to the Map.
